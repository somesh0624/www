My Favorite Places

Discover some of my favorite destinations across Malaysia that I appreciate for their vibe, food, and culture.

1 • Hometown & Island City

George Town

My hometown. A world-class food paradise filled with rich heritage, iconic street art, and island scenery.

2 • Capital City

Kuala Lumpur

A modern skyline dominated by goated skyscrapers, vibrant city lights, and endless urban spots to explore.

3 • Hill Station

Cameron Highlands

The coldest place with fresh mountain air, scenic tea plantations, and a relaxing cool breeze.

4 • Heritage City

Ipoh

Famous for its historic Old Town, expressive street art murals, limestone caves, and legendary local coffee.

5 • Resort City

Genting Highlands

The ultimate city of entertainment featuring cool mountain breezes, theme parks, and vibrant night spots.

6 • Southern Hub

Johor Bahru

A lively state with great shopping destinations, vibrant cafe culture, and cool coastal hangouts.
